About the Farm

Rustic Acres Farm is run by Todd Olden, focused on raising animals with care, patience, and respect for the land. We believe good farming starts with good stewardship and a commitment to doing things the right way.

Our farm centers on pasture-raised meats, along with eggs and seasonal vegetables, all produced using simple, non-GMO practices. Animals are fed non-GMO feed, and everything is raised with an emphasis on quality, transparency, and respect for both the animals and the land.

At Rustic Acres, farming is meant to feel personal. That means knowing where your food comes from, how it was raised, and who raised it. The goal is to provide food you can trust, produced with care from start to finish.

Whether you’re visiting for the first time, picking up farm products, or stopping by for an event, we’re glad to share a little piece of the farm with you.
